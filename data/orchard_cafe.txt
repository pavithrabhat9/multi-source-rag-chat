Orchard Cafe - Visitor Information

Orchard Cafe is open every day from 8 am to 6 pm, except on Mondays when it is closed.
The cafe is famous for its cinnamon apple tart, which costs 6 euros.
Free Wi-Fi is available. The Wi-Fi password is "applepie42".
Dogs are welcome on the outdoor terrace but not inside.
Reservations are only needed for groups of 8 or more people.
