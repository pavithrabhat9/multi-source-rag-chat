Lumora Labs - Product Handbook

Lumora Labs was founded in 2019 in Lisbon, Portugal by Marta Silva, who is still the CEO.
The company makes smart desk lamps.

The flagship product is the Lumora Lamp X2. It costs 49 dollars.
The X2 battery lasts 12 hours on a full charge and charges through USB-C in 90 minutes.
Every Lumora Lamp comes with a 24 month warranty. The warranty does not cover water damage.

The older model, the Lumora Lamp X1, was discontinued in 2022 and is no longer sold.
Customer support is available by email only, Monday to Friday.
