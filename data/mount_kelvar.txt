Mount Kelvar - Hiking Guide

Mount Kelvar is 3412 meters high and is the tallest peak in the fictional Varda range.
The main summit trail is 14 kilometers long and takes about 7 hours to climb.
The best season to hike is from June to September. Snow closes the trail in winter.
A day permit costs 15 euros and must be bought at the Eastgate ranger station.
Camping is allowed only at Lake Orin, about halfway up the trail.
